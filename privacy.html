Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Welcome to Qmegle. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Qmegle website and its random video and text chat services.

1. Information We Collect

Qmegle is designed to allow users to start random conversations without requiring a traditional account or registration.

Depending on how the service is used, technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, connection information, and basic server logs may potentially be processed for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

2. Camera and Microphone

Qmegle may request permission to access your device camera and microphone when you start a video chat.

Camera and microphone access is controlled by your browser. You can deny or revoke these permissions through your browser settings.

3. Video and Audio Communication

Qmegle uses real-time web communication technology to establish video and audio connections between users.

Users should understand that communicating with strangers online always involves privacy and safety risks.

4. Chat Messages

Qmegle provides text chat functionality during conversations. Users should avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, financial information, private documents, addresses, or other sensitive personal information with strangers.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Qmegle may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, analytics, or future service improvements.

6. Third-Party Services

Qmegle may use third-party infrastructure or services to operate the website, provide hosting, networking, security, analytics, or other technical functionality.

7. Children's Privacy

Qmegle is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to legally use online communication services in their jurisdiction.

If you are not legally permitted to use this type of service, please do not use Qmegle.

8. User Safety

Never share sensitive personal information with strangers. If another user behaves inappropriately, use the Report feature provided by Qmegle.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect the service and its technical infrastructure. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Qmegle develops new features or changes how the service operates. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
