Free Video Chat Online

Qmegle is a free online video chat platform designed to help people meet new users through random conversations. You can access Qmegle from a supported web browser and start a conversation without installing a separate application.

When you start a chat, Qmegle can use your device camera and microphone to establish a real-time video and audio connection with another available user.

🎥 Start Free Video Chat

How Free Video Chat Works

Using Qmegle is simple. Open the platform, select Start Chat and allow the camera and microphone permissions requested by your browser. Qmegle then looks for another available user.

Once a match is available, you can communicate through video, audio and text chat. If you want another conversation, the Next button can be used to search for another available user.

Use Video Chat on Mobile

Qmegle can be accessed from supported mobile browsers. Video chat may require permission to access your phone's camera and microphone.

Stay Safe While Video Chatting

Never share passwords, OTPs, banking information, private documents or your home address with strangers. If another user behaves inappropriately, stop the conversation and use the available report option.
