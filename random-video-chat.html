What Is Random Video Chat?

Random video chat is a way to meet and communicate with people online through live video conversations. Instead of choosing a specific person, a random matching system can connect you with another available user.

Qmegle is designed to make this experience simple. You can visit the platform, start a chat and wait while Qmegle looks for another available user.

How Qmegle Random Video Chat Works

  1. Open Qmegle and select Start Chat.
  2. Allow camera and microphone permissions when your browser asks for them.
  3. Qmegle searches for another available user.
  4. When a match is available, the video connection can begin.
  5. You can use text chat during the conversation.
  6. Use Next if you want to look for another conversation.
