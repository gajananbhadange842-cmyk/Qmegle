Chat With Strangers Online

Qmegle lets you chat with strangers online through random video and text conversations. The platform is designed for people who want to discover new conversations directly from a web browser.

Instead of choosing a specific person, Qmegle's random matching system can connect you with another available user. Each new conversation can be different.

💬 Start Chatting

How to Chat With New People

Click Start Chat on Qmegle and follow your browser's permission requests if you want to use video and audio. The platform then searches for another available user.

You can use text chat during a conversation and move to another conversation with the Next button.

Respect Other Users

Random conversations work best when everyone communicates respectfully. Do not harass, threaten, scam or share inappropriate content with other users.

Protect Your Personal Information

Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, financial information, private documents, addresses or other sensitive information with strangers.
