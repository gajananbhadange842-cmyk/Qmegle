About Qmegle

Welcome to Qmegle, a free random video and text chat platform designed to help people meet and communicate with new people online.

What is Qmegle?

Qmegle provides a simple way to start a random online conversation. Users can start a video chat and, when another available user is found, communicate through video and text chat.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make online conversations simple and accessible. Qmegle is designed to provide an easy-to-use platform where people can discover new conversations without complicated registration processes.

Random Video Chat

Qmegle uses real-time web technology to connect users for random video conversations. Users can start a chat, move to another conversation using the Next button, or stop a conversation at any time.

Safety Comes First

Users should always protect their personal information when talking with strangers online. Never share passwords, OTPs, bank information, private documents, or other sensitive information with unknown people.

If you experience inappropriate behavior, use the Report feature available on Qmegle.

Free to Use

Qmegle is designed as a free random video and text chat platform. You can visit Qmegle and start exploring random conversations online.

For questions, feedback, or other inquiries, please visit our Contact Us page.
