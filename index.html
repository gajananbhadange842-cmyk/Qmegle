What Is Qmegle?

Qmegle is a free random video chat platform that allows you to meet new people online. You can start a conversation with a random stranger using video, audio and text chat.

How Does Random Video Chat Work?

Simply click the Start Chat button and Qmegle will look for another available user. Once a stranger is found, the two users can communicate through video and text chat.

Why Use Qmegle?

  • Free random video chat
  • Meet new people online
  • Simple and easy interface
  • Works on desktop and mobile devices
  • Text chat available during conversations

Stay Safe While Chatting

Never share passwords, banking information, private addresses or other sensitive personal information with strangers online. If another user behaves inappropriately, use the Report button and end the conversation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Qmegle free?

Yes. Qmegle is designed as a free random video chat platform.

Can I use Qmegle on my phone?

Yes. Qmegle's responsive interface is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and computers.

Do I need an account?

Qmegle can connect users without requiring a traditional account registration.

Can I skip a stranger?

Yes. Use the Next button to leave the current conversation and search for another stranger.

How can I report someone?

During a conversation, click the Report button and submit the reason for your report.
