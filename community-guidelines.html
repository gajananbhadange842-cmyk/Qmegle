Qmegle Community Guidelines

Welcome to Qmegle

Qmegle is a platform that allows people to meet and communicate with new people online. Our goal is to provide a friendly, respectful, and safe environment for everyone.

By using Qmegle, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other people with respect. Remember that there is a real person on the other side of the conversation.

  • Do not harass or bully other users.
  • Do not threaten other people.
  • Do not intentionally insult or humiliate others.
  • Respect different opinions and backgrounds.

2. No Harassment

Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact is not allowed.

If another user makes you uncomfortable, you should leave the conversation and use the available reporting features when appropriate.

3. No Sexual or Explicit Content

Qmegle should not be used to share or request sexually explicit, pornographic, or inappropriate content.

  • Do not send explicit images or videos.
  • Do not request sexual content from other users.
  • Do not use the platform for sexual exploitation.

4. Protect Your Privacy

Never share sensitive personal information with strangers.

  • Passwords
  • Bank or payment information
  • Private identification documents
  • Home address
  • Other sensitive personal information
Safety Tip: Be careful when communicating with people you have never met in real life.

5. No Scams or Fraud

Users must not use Qmegle to scam, deceive, defraud, or manipulate other people.

  • No fake investment schemes.
  • No phishing attempts.
  • No requests for money through deception.
  • No impersonation intended to deceive others.

6. No Illegal Activities

Qmegle must not be used to facilitate or promote illegal activities.

7. No Harmful or Dangerous Content

Do not use Qmegle to encourage violence, serious harm, or dangerous activities against other people.

8. Do Not Spam

Avoid sending repeated unwanted messages, advertisements, malicious links, or automated spam.

9. Report Problems

If you encounter inappropriate, abusive, threatening, or suspicious behavior, use Qmegle's reporting feature when available.

You can also leave the conversation if you feel unsafe.

10. User Responsibility

Qmegle users are responsible for their own interactions. Never assume that a stranger online is who they claim to be.

11. Enforcement

Qmegle may take appropriate action against accounts or users that violate these guidelines, including limiting or terminating access to the service.

12. Changes to These Guidelines

These Community Guidelines may be updated from time to time to improve safety and the overall Qmegle experience.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about these guidelines, please visit our Contact Us page.
